# Facts on screen

The narration is the user's script. These are the numbers and labels the picture adds, and where they come from.
Estimates for the Jurassic vary; the film shows ranges or rounded values where the script does.

| On screen | Basis |
|---|---|
| 150,000,000 years in the past; Late Jurassic | The Morrison Formation is dated about 157–148 Ma (Kimmeridgian–Tithonian). |
| Morrison Formation across Montana, Wyoming, Utah, Colorado | As in the script; the outcrop shape on the map is approximate and hand-drawn. |
| CO₂ dial: today ≈ 420 ppm, Jurassic needle ≈ 1,800 ppm ("several times today") | Proxy reconstructions for the Late Jurassic mostly fall around 1,000–2,000+ ppm (e.g. Foster, Royer & Lunt 2017). |
| O₂ dial that won't settle between less and more than today | Models disagree (GEOCARBSULF-type models vs charcoal-based estimates), as the script says. |
| ≈ 6 L per person per day in heat; 600 L a day for 100 | The script's "several liters a day", taken as about 6 L (military planning for hot climates is 6–12 L). |
| Cycads: liver and nervous system; ferns and horsetails destroy vitamin B1 | Cycasin and BMAA in cycads; thiaminase in bracken ferns and horsetails. |
| Diplodocus about 25 m; school bus about 12 m; elephants | Diplodocus carnegii is about 24–26 m long; a full-size US school bus is about 12 m. |
| Tail-tip speed: 1990s supersonic proposal vs a 2022 model below Mach 1 | Myhrvold & Currie 1997 (Paleobiology); Conti et al. 2022 (Scientific Reports) estimated about 30 m/s. |
| Allosaurus 8–9 m; most common large predator | Allosaurus fragilis adults are about 8.5 m. |
| Cleveland-Lloyd Dinosaur Quarry, Utah; at least 46 Allosaurus | Minimum number of individuals reported for the quarry (e.g. Gates 2005; Peterson et al. 2017). |
| Big Al, Wyoming, 1991; 19 injuries | MOR 693; Hanna 2002 (Journal of Vertebrate Paleontology). |
| Stegosaurus spike matched a hole in an Allosaurus tail vertebra | Carpenter, Sanders, McWhinney & Wood 2005. |
| Ornitholestes about the size of a large dog | About 2 m long, about 12 kg. |
| Rounds per soldier counting down from 210 | A standard US basic load for a rifleman is 210 rounds (7 × 30-round magazines). |
