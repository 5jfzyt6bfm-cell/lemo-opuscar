# Facts used in the film

Figures are rounded and simplified for a 30-second piece. Estimates of ancient air vary between models.

| Claim in the film | Basis |
|---|---|
| The Mesozoic is 252–66 million years ago | International Chronostratigraphic Chart (ICS): Triassic base 251.9 Ma, Cretaceous–Paleogene boundary 66.0 Ma. |
| "Two hundred million years ago" | Triassic–Jurassic boundary, 201.4 Ma (ICS chart). |
| Oxygen about 16% then vs 21% today | GEOCARBSULF-type models (Berner 2006, 2009) put late-Triassic/early-Jurassic O₂ at roughly 15–17%; other models differ. |
| Feels like ~2.5 km altitude | Breathing 16% O₂ at sea level gives about the same oxygen partial pressure as 21% O₂ at roughly 2,500–2,700 m (ICAO standard atmosphere). |
| 40°C heat | Climate models and evidence for the Pangaean interior show summer highs above 40°C (e.g. Sellwood & Valdes 2006). |
| About three days without water | The common survival rule of thumb (roughly 3 days); in strong heat it can be shorter. |
| Dinosaurs lasted about 165 million years | Earliest dinosaurs ~233 Ma (Late Triassic) to the end-Cretaceous extinction at 66 Ma (non-avian dinosaurs). |
