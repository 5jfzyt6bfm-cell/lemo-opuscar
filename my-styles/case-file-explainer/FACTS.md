# Facts in "Attention That Won't Steer"

| On screen | Source | Note |
|---|---|---|
| "It's not a lack of attention. It's attention that won't steer." | CHADD / National Resource Center on ADHD, "About ADHD", and R. A. Barkley's description of ADHD as a disorder of self-regulation | Mainstream clinical framing: difficulty *regulating* attention rather than an absence of it. Paraphrased for a caption |
| Hyperfocus (4 hours on one video topic) | Hupfeld, Abagis & Shah (2019), "Living 'in the zone': hyperfocus in adult ADHD", *ADHD Attention Deficit and Hyperactivity Disorders* 11, 191–208 | Maya's 4 hours is a story event, not a statistic |
| "About 1 in 20 kids has ADHD." | Polanczyk et al. (2007), *American Journal of Psychiatry* 164:942–948: worldwide pooled prevalence 5.29% under 18; Polanczyk et al. (2014), *Int. J. Epidemiology* 43:434–442, no rise over three decades | 5.3% ≈ 1 in 19; rounded to "about 1 in 20" |
