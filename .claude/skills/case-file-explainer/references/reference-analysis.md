## What every reference shares
- One ordinary person stands in for the topic; the study arrives only after the story.
- Captions are the narration, a few words at a time, one accent word.
- One big typographic event every few seconds (stamp, giant number, counter, clock).
- Dark, cinematic, glowing worlds; figures always the brightest thing.
- The picture often says the opposite of the words.
